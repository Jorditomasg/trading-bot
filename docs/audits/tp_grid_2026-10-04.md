# TP grid > 5.0 — hypothesis test (2026-10-04)

Open item #2 of `strategy_review_2026-08-15.md`: Calmar was still rising at the
TP grid edge (1.53 at TP 6.0) and `walk_forward.TP_GRID` stops at 5.0.

**Hypothesis:** TP > 5.0 ATR improves Calmar out-of-sample without raising DD.

**Method:** `scripts/test_tp_grid.py` — true live config via
`build_backtest_config` (stress_test_2026 harness), stop 1.5, TP ∈ {4,5,6,7,8},
klines refreshed to 2026-10-04. Full 2017→now BTC+ETH, calendar years, and a
rolling walk-forward: train on the previous 3 years, pick the TP with the best
Calmar, test on the next year. ~30 min on 4 workers.

**Verdict: REJECT — keep TP 5.0.**

- The surface above 5.0 is **noise, not a plateau**: Calmar 1.33 / 1.35 / 1.13 /
  1.36 for TP 5/6/7/8. A real edge would not dip at 7.0 and recover at 8.0.
- The walk-forward pick **does not beat fixed 5.0**: ~2.21x vs ~2.30x
  compounded 2020-2026. Optimizing TP each year adds churn, not return.
- TP 8.0 has the best PF (1.64) but lower Sharpe (1.29 vs 1.31), higher DD
  (13.4% vs 12.2%) and win rate 27% — longer losing streaks for the same Calmar.
- TP 4.0 is clearly worse on everything (Calmar 0.80): the lower edge is real.

No need to widen `walk_forward.TP_GRID`.
